Environments

The estate is split into four tiers. A build candidate moves left to right: it is cut in dev, soaked in staging, signed off in grading, and exercised against a production-shaped fixture in prod-sim before a bundle is cut on the release channel.

TierHostnamePurpose
devdev.meridian.localDeveloper integration. Candidates are cut here and pushed to the artifact cache. Freely re-deployed; nothing here is authoritative.
stagingstaging.meridian.localSoak and smoke tests before a candidate is offered to the grading runners. Mirrors prod-sim config but with scratch data.
gradinggrading.meridian.localWhere grading runners fetch candidates and record sign-offs. The verdict of record is held by the grading authority on the management network, not here.
prod-simprod-sim.meridian.localProduction-shaped fixture used to exercise a promoted build before the bundle is published. Read-only to the build estate.
